Privacy notice — mac-setup Google access

This page covers the OAuth client "mac-setup google access", used by the mac-setup project: a personal, self-hosted agent setup (Hermes Agent) running on the owner's own hardware.

What data is accessed

  • Gmail (read-only): the owner's own inboxes — search, listing and reading messages.
  • Google Calendar (read-only): the owner's own calendars — listing events and free/busy.
  • Identity: the account's name and email address, so each inbox is addressed to its owner.

The client requests no write scopes. Sending mail or modifying calendars is disabled in the setup; no other Google data (Drive, Contacts, Chat, …) is requested.

Who can see it

This client is used solely by the owner of those accounts, on their own machine and a private virtual machine reachable only from that household's network. There is no multi-user access, no third-party service in the data path (except Google itself), and nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.

Storage

OAuth tokens for the owner's accounts are stored only on that private machine, in files readable by the VM user. Replies generated by the agent stay with the owner (local chat sessions / their own messaging apps).

Contact & revocation

Questions or data concerns: felix.lung@gmail.com. Access can be revoked at any time in Google Account → Security → "Third-party apps with account access", or by deleting the consent grant on Google's account permissions page.
